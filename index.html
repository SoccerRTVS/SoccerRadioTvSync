Privacy Policy

Soccer RadioTv Sync is designed to operate without user accounts and without collecting personal information.

Information collected by the app

Soccer RadioTv Sync does not collect or sell personal data. The app does not display advertising, use analytics or tracking technologies, or include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Internet access and radio streams

The app uses an internet connection to play radio streams, check whether stream URLs are reachable, and search for publicly listed radio stations through Radio Browser. Streams may come from URLs or playlists added by the user or from stations discovered through the integrated search.

Radio streams are delivered by independent third-party providers. When the app connects to Radio Browser or a stream provider, those services may receive standard technical information associated with an internet request, such as the device's IP address and user-agent information. Their handling of that information is governed by their own practices and policies.

Radio Browser searches

Search terms and selected filters, such as country, are sent directly to Radio Browser servers only to retrieve matching public radio stations. Soccer RadioTv Sync does not store a history of these searches or use them for analytics, advertising, profiling, or user tracking.

The app does not use Radio Browser's optional endpoints for reporting station clicks, playback statistics, or votes.

In-App Purchases

Premium purchases are processed through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Soccer RadioTv Sync does not collect, receive, or store payment card details. Apple processes payments and provides the app only with the transaction information needed to determine access to Premium features.

Information stored on the device

Station URLs, playlists, language preferences, background settings, purchase entitlement status, and other app preferences may be stored locally on the user's device to provide the app's features and preserve the user's settings.

Soccer RadioTv Sync does not store audio recordings.

Radio content

Soccer RadioTv Sync does not own, host, or provide radio broadcasts. Radio Browser supplies public directory information, while the streams themselves are operated by third parties. Users should only add or select public or authorized sources and remain responsible for their use of those sources.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page with its revision date.
